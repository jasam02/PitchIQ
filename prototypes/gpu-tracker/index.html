    Match events

    Goals, substitutions, restarts and stoppages found in the full match, also marked on the timeline under the video. Reviewing them is optional: confirm, correct or add one where it helps, and Undo any correction.

    Open a full match to review its events.

    Match identities

    Select an identity to highlight it on every view and jump to its first observation.

    Full match: the roster review shows, per team, who holds each on-field slot (01–10 and GK) at the playback time, that person's match-wide id, when they were last observed and how sure the slot is, the slot's substitutions, the fragments no slot claims and the ones left out. Correct a slot from its row; every view follows at once.

    Corrections

    Click a person's box on the tracking video to change who they are: team A or B player, goalkeeper, or not a player. Every frame of that person is relabelled, and later runs recognise them by appearance and keep your change.

    Full match: the corrections of the match identities. They are stored with the match, apart from the tracking; Undo removes one.

    Result

    Run a segment to inspect detections and identities. No accuracy score is claimed.

    Global identities persist while players leave and return; when the evidence is unclear a person stays identity uncertain instead of being guessed. Referees are tracked separately. The ball is one persistent track, predicted while hidden and unknown when it cannot be confirmed.

    Debug overlays

    Identity debug (on the tracking controls) adds the per-person details and the pitch confidence notes on the video. Registered pitch draws the frame's final homography, the geometry the 2D positions come from: cyan, sides solid where paint verified them and dashed where predicted, the other markings dashed, the detected paint thin white; muted below confidence 0.5.

    Identity events

    Newest first. Click an event to jump to it. Scores are heuristic matching confidence, not calibrated probabilities.

    Run a segment to see identity decisions.

    Pitch confirmation

    Stretches where no painted boundary line and no calibration hold the pitch model are grouped here. Confirming one pauses the video on its most useful frame with the predicted landmarks placed (drag the hollow points onto the real lines), lets you add more, and saves a calibration keyframe for the next run.

    Pitch calibration keyframes

    For real pitch coordinates, pause on a clear frame, pick a landmark and click it on the video; mark at least four that span an area. Add another keyframe after big camera moves or cuts.

    Field boundary keyframes

    The playable area is detected automatically. For a neighbouring pitch or unusual turf, pause on a clear frame and draw a polygon around this match's playing area.
