Field boundary keyframes (optional)

The playable area is detected automatically on every frame from grass and the touchlines. For a neighbouring pitch or unusual turf, pause on a clear frame and draw a polygon around this match's playing area; it is combined with the automatic one (the stricter wins) and follows camera motion until the next keyframe.

Pitch calibration keyframes (optional)

For real pitch coordinates (x, y on a 105×68 m pitch), pause on a clear frame, pick a landmark and click it on the video; mark at least four that span an area (not on one line). The calibration follows pans and zooms until the camera motion becomes unreliable; add another keyframe after big moves or cuts.
