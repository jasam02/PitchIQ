2D match view

The 2D match view appears here after a run.

Top-down recreation of the match from the saved tracking result, in step with the video: players as team-coloured circles at the pitch position of their feet (through the landmark calibration), goalkeepers marked GK, the ball as a small white circle (dashed while predicted). Positions exist only where the pitch is calibrated; nobody is placed by guesswork, and referees, bench and crowd are left out.

Field boundary keyframes (optional)

The playable area is detected automatically on every frame from grass and the touchlines. For a neighbouring pitch or unusual turf, pause on a clear frame and draw a polygon around this match's playing area; it is combined with the automatic one (the stricter wins) and follows camera motion until the next keyframe.

Pitch calibration keyframes (optional)

For real pitch coordinates (x, y on a 105×68 m pitch), pause on a clear frame, pick a landmark and click it on the video; mark at least four that span an area (not on one line). The calibration follows pans and zooms until the camera motion becomes unreliable; add another keyframe after big moves or cuts.

Global identities persist while players leave and return; when the evidence is unclear a person stays identity uncertain instead of being guessed. Referees are tracked separately and are not players; goalkeepers are shown by team (GK-A, GK-B). Off-screen positions remain unknown. The ball is one persistent track (BALL-1) followed through time: while hidden its position is predicted (dashed), and when it cannot be confirmed it is BALL UNKNOWN instead of a guess.
