MOMENT sheet: what happened at this moment?

Instructions

Each row is one moment of a match video. Press Play: the clip runs from 3 s before the moment and stops on it; the pink rings then mark the players of the team the row asks about (their tracking labels are in the "Team" column). Play on shows the next 2 s. Answer every question that applies; choose "not applicable" when the question is about the other phase (for example "pressure" while this team had the ball), and "unclear" when the video does not show enough. The three "raise" questions ask whether a coach of that age band would point out a positioning problem of this team at this moment.

Do not open PitchIQ's coaching report for these videos before you finish: the sheet is blind on purpose. Your answers save in this browser as you go; Download answers writes moment_labels.csv (give it to the scorer).
